How to Use

  • Click "Start Sentence" to begin building a sentence
  • Click "Start Word" to begin forming a custom word
  • Make sign language gestures in front of your camera
  • Click "End Word" to add the completed word to your sentence
  • Click "End Sentence" when finished
  • Use "Speak Sentence" to hear the translated sentence
  • Use "Speak Word" to hear the current word being built
  • Use "Next Gesture" to clear the current gesture and prepare for the next one
